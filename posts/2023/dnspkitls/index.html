DNS, PKI, TLS - 03/21/23

March 23, 2023 · 1 min

For the weekly meeting on Tuesday, March 21st 2023, the Ethical Hacking Club’s president, Tyler Ray, presented a talk on DNS, PKI, and TLS Certificates. If you don’t already know these acronyms, they stand for Domain Name System, Public Key Infrastructure, and Transport Layer Security. These three topics are integrated together and are crucial for building one’s cybersecurity foundational knowledge.

If you missed the meeting, review the slides below to learn about DNS, PKI, and TLS Certificates.
