Cryptography Spring 2023

February 21, 2023 · 1 min

On February 21st, 2023, the ethical hacking club’s weekly meeting was focused on cryptography, led by technical advisor Lyndsey Rice. Following the lecture was a DIY practice lab time on cryptohack.org. Accounts on the website are free to create and use, and the site is full of bite-sized lessons and challenges relating to cryptography and hacking. The evening was a great chance for members of the club to explore various encryption techniques and learn how they are used (and misued) in modern technology. Further resources for learning about cryptography are linked at the end of the slide deck below.
