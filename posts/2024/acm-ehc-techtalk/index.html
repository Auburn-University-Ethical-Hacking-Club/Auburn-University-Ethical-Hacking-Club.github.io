ACM/EHC TechTalk

April 22, 2024 · 1 min

This Wednesday the ACRC will be having guest speakers Michelle Leger and Will Zortman from Sandia National Labs give a lecture! Description is as follows:

ACM / EHC Tech Talk sponsored by the Auburn Cyber Research Center (ACRC) Sandia’s Digital Assurance for High Consequence Systems (DAHCS)
The digital foundations and security consequences of modern integrated systems

Short Abstract : This talk will explore a framework for thinking about open questions in the technical foundations for understanding digital system behavior in the face of threats, using evidence, abstractions, modeling, and system composition to make reasonable claims about a system’s digital assurance.

This event will be by reservation and reservations close Monday (tonight!) night so don’t wait!
Link To Sign Up:https://aub.ie/ACM-SNL

Details:

  • When: Wednesday, April 24 from 5:00PM to 6:00PM
  • Where: In-Person in Shelby 3129
  • Food: There will be free food! But since supplies are limited, make sure you sign up if you want to come!
