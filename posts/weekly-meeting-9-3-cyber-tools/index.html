Weekly Meeting 9/3: Cyber Tools

September 3, 2024 · 1 min

This week we will continue with topics for people getting into cyber by introducing some relevant tools that are used in industry, education, and competitions. The meeting will be led collaboratively by Marshall, Luke, and Jon who will share their experiences and know-how when it comes to using some of these tools. If you are interested in participating in any of the events/competitions we will be hosting this semester, or if you are curious about what tools are relevant to previous competitions you have participated in, we highly recommend you attend.

There may be some new announcements about competitions this week, so if you are interested please attend!

Per usual, this meeting will be held tonight from 5PM to 6PM in Shelby 3129.
