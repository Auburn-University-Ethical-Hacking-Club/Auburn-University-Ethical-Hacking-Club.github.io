## What is DownUnderCTF?

DUCTF is a worldwide CTF primarily targeted at Australian schools. Seven challenge categories with difficulties ranging from "sanity check" to "insane" were available last year, covering OSINT, forensics, web, crypto, misc, pwn, and reversing. We expect the categories to be similar this year.

## When is it?

Start: 24th September 2021 at 4:00 AM CST (7:00 PM AEST)  
End: 26th September 2021 at 4:00 AM CST (7:00 PM AEST)

## Prizes?

Prizes are available only for Australian students.
