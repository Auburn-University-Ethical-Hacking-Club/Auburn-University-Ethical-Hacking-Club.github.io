Join EHC and the Los Alamos National Laboratory on the weekend of August 21st to 23rd for AU Cyberfire 2026!

### What is Cyberfire?

Cyberfire is a ctf-style hacking competition inviting players of all skill levels. Whether this is your first time participating in a competition or you're a seasoned player, Cyberfire is an excellent oppertunity to learn, compete and connect with like-minded students and professionals.


### What is Included?

You're registration fee covers these meals throughout the 3-day event:

    - Friday: Dinner
    - Saturday: Lunch, Dinner & Snacks
    - Sunday: Lunch & Snacks

### What's the cost to attend?

    - Early Bird (If you register before Wednesday, August 19th at 12:00 PM): $30 or $25 if you are a dues-paying member of EHC.
    - Normal Pricing: $35 or $30 if you are a dues-paying member of EHC.
