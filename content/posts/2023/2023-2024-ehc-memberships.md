## For All New and Returning Members!

Starting in Fall 2023 Auburn’s Ethical Hacking Club is collecting dues*. Dues are $10 a person and will automatically make you a member of EHC. 

#### Membership provides many benefits such as:

  * Reduced entry fees for events and tours.
  * Priority registration for events and tours when there are limited spots available. 
  * Reception of a special Discord role as proof of membership.
  * The ability to purchase cords for graduation! 

See the attached pdf for more information. Register today! At [aub.ie/acm-dues](aub.ie/acm-dues)

*_You can still participate in EHC without paying dues, but you will miss out on the extra benefits_
