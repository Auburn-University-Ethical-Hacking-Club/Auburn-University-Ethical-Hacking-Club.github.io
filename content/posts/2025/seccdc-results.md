Hello everyone, over this past weekend EHC participated in the South Eastern Collegiate Cyber Defense Competition (SECCDC) and achieved great results with a final placing of 11th out of 38.

According to the [CCDC website](https://www.nationalccdc.org/index.php/competition/about-ccdc/mission) the mission of the competition is to:

_"provide institutions with an information assurance or computer security curriculum a controlled, competitive environment to assess their student's depth  of understanding and operational competency in managing the challenges inherent in protecting a corporate network infrastructure and business information systems."_

We are proud of our participants and hope they had fun!
