Tuesday, 02/28, EHC welcomed guest speaker Demarcus, an AU Software Engineering alum, to speak to club members on the topic of penetration testing. Demarcus is currently working as a full time professional pentester, and his lecture was a practical and insightful talk on what this profession looks like in the real world. He discussed the concept of C2 (Command and Control) for post-exploitation, and gave examples of tools that can be used for this. 

Thanks for visiting, Demarcus!
