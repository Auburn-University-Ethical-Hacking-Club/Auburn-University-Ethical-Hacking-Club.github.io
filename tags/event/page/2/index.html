Cyber Fire Puzzles 2023

What is Cyber Fire Puzzles? Created by the U.S. Department of Energy and Los Alamos National Laboratory, Cyber Fire Puzzles is a friendly competition that features a plethora of interesting mathematical and computer-science-based problems. You can expect to see sequence problems (think 1,5,9,d,…), number base conversion, network forensics, reverse engineering, and more. What is Tracer FIRE Sandia National Laboratories (https://www.sandia.gov) will be covering incident response, and having participants group up to work through a mock incident. This event mocks an actual compromised network and have participants investigate how the network was compromised, how adversaries moved around the network, and what was stolen from the network. SECCDC 2023

In early February 2023, several members of EHC competed in the Southeastern Collegiate Cyber Defense Challenge Regionals round, a popular blue teaming competition where teams are tasked with protecting a collection of vulnerable machines from professional red teamers who volunteer their time to wreak havoc on university students’ attempts to beat them. The competition lasted several hours and became increasingly chaotic, imitating what a multi-hour external attack on systems could look like. Mixed into the protection of systems, however, are customer requests from the fictional company that teams “work” for in the scenario. ...
