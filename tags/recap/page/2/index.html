The second meeting started off with a simple discussion on Wireless Security Protocols, from OSA to WPA2. Then we went over the various ways to crack into these protocols using Aircrack-ng. History of Wireless Security OSA vs SKA (1997) When wireless was first becoming something worth looking at, security was in the very back of the mind of the innovators, therefore, the first protocols that came out for authentication were near useless. Open System Association, or OSA, was a complete free-for-all, similar to “open” wireless networks today.
